先確認號碼的官方類別及是否已配予車輛。自訂、普通及特殊登記號碼的規則不同，不能只憑字樣推斷。投得號碼後亦有指定配車期限；未有車輛時，應先向運輸署核對。

First confirm the official mark type and whether it is assigned to a vehicle. Personalized, ordinary and special marks have different rules; lettering alone is not a legal classification. Auctioned marks also have assignment deadlines; check with TD before bidding if you do not have a vehicle.
