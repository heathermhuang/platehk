### QR root cause

- The vendored QR generator threw during `qr.make()`.
- `PAD0` and `PAD1` were declared inside the QR constructor, but the `createData()` helper that pads the QR payload lives outside that constructor scope.
- The poster code caught the thrown error and rendered fallback URL text inside the QR panel, so the poster appeared to have a broken/non-scannable QR code.

### QR fix

- [assets/vendor/qrcode-generator.js](/Users/heatherm/Documents/Codex/PVRM/assets/vendor/qrcode-generator.js)
  - Moved `PAD0` and `PAD1` into the outer generator scope so `createData()` can read them.
- [assets/index.share.js](/Users/heatherm/Documents/Codex/PVRM/assets/index.share.js)
  - Increased QR quiet zone to 4 modules.
  - Kept the QR canvas at the target poster size and centered integer-sized modules.
  - Draws the QR at native size instead of stretching it into the poster canvas.
- [index.html](/Users/heatherm/Documents/Codex/PVRM/index.html)
  - Bumped cache-busting versions for `qrcode-generator.js` and `index.share.js` to `20260503-01`.
