## Design Context

### Users
People inspecting Hong Kong vehicle-registration-mark auction records in Traditional Chinese or English, on desktop and mobile. Their core tasks are finding a mark, comparing dated auction results, and checking the original official source.

### Brand Personality
Precise, composed, local. Confidence comes from readable evidence and source traceability. Preserve the Ledger palette, the yellow registration-mark identity, and the existing Chinese/Latin typography.

### Aesthetic Direction
A refined data publication with the practical density of a research tool. The records provide the visual identity. Keep source-verified round summaries in the auction-results archive; the record browser stays focused on the current query. Avoid promotional heroes, decorative metrics, arbitrary font swaps and generic feature-card layouts.

### Design Principles
1. Make the plate, auction amount, date and original source easy to scan together.
2. Group search and filtering controls with a clear visual and keyboard order.
3. Use space for hierarchy while keeping useful data within the initial viewport.
4. Keep all official-source distinctions, date precision and plate layouts intact.
5. Judge visual and interaction quality in the rendered interface as well as through tests.
